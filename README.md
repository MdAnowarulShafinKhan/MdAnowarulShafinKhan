I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate from the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), Bangladesh, working in computational materials science and atomistic modeling.

## Selected Research

### Graphene/MoSe₂/Graphene Trilayer — Tensile Failure

LAMMPS molecular dynamics study of temperature- and direction-dependent tensile behavior, mechanical properties, load sharing, and layer-resolved fracture in a graphene/MoSe₂/graphene van der Waals heterostructure.

**Status:** Manuscript under review

### Janus MoSeTe — Vacancy, Strain & Fracture

Coupled molecular dynamics study of face-selective vacancies, pre-strain, tensile deformation, fracture, thermal transport, and vibrational behavior in Janus MoSeTe monolayers.

**Status:** Research in progress

### h-AlN — Strain, Nanopore & Substitution Engineering

NEMD study of strain, nanopores, B/Ga substitution, temperature and finite-size effects, anisotropic thermal transport, uncertainty analysis, and directional/species-resolved vibrational density of states.

**Status:** Manuscript under review

### h-AlN/MoS₂ van der Waals Heterostructures

Atomistic investigation of thermal transport in h-AlN/MoS₂ bilayers, including finite-size and temperature effects, defects, strain, interface-mediated phonon scattering, vibrational coupling, and **interfacial thermal resistance (ITR)**.

Ongoing work also investigates twist-angle and moiré effects on interlayer phonon coupling and in-plane thermal transport.

**Status:** Research in progress

### GaS, GaSe & InSe — Defect and Strain Engineering

Comparative NEMD study of vacancy-, strain-, size-, and direction-dependent thermal transport, effective transport length, and vibrational behavior in III-VI monolayers.

**Status:** Manuscript in preparation

## Current Research Direction
